■ Critical 1. Personal data stored in clear text in the landing layer

Sold_MARICHIM holds names, birth dates (years 1943–2000), ID numbers, street, house number, zip, mobile phone and email for 211 people. ■ Critical 2. Grades cannot be linked to notebooks for 5 of 6 exam sessions

Sold_Harachot covers sessions 162–172, but Sold_MACHBAROT holds only session 163. For session 163 every grade row matches (0 orphans of 43,765). Overall 2,478,833 of 2,522,598 rows (98.3%) have no notebook. Affects: 2,478,833 rows · Decision needed: Where do notebooks for sessions 162 and 164–172 come from? Is the 9540 NotebookID a different key than Harachot.MAC_REC_ID?

■ Critical 3. Grader IDs do not match the grader master

2,453,802 of 2,522,598 Sold_Harachot rows (97.3%) carry a Tz_Maarikh not found in Sold_MARICHIM (211 people, session 163 only). Sold_MACHBAROT.Inspector_TZ and Sold_MANOT.TzMetapel match nobody (100% orphans), although both sides are 9-digit IDs.

Affects: 2,477,199 rows · Decision needed: Is Sold_MARICHIM the full grader list? Are inspectors and handlers a different population that needs its own master?

MRR_MagenGrade.SEMEL_SHEELON (237 codes, mostly 4–6 digits) matches none of the 726 Sold_SHELONIM codes (6–7 digits): 903,247 rows. Sold_MANOT_9540.SemelShelon: 17,339 rows, 100% orphans.
